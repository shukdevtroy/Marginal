BEFORE YOU WRITE

Quick fixes for the usual suspects.

Most problems come down to one of these. Try them first and you may be done in a minute.

The Marginal panel doesn't appear in Gmail

Refresh your Gmail tab after installing or reloading the extension, then open a new compose window. Click the Marginal icon in your toolbar, pick a provider, and press Save & test to confirm it connects.

Ollama says it can't connect

Ollama needs permission to talk to the extension. Set OLLAMA_ORIGINS to chrome-extension://* (on Windows with setx, on macOS with launchctl setenv), then quit and reopen Ollama. The full steps are in the setup guide.

My AI provider stopped responding

Free tiers have daily or monthly limits. Set a backup provider under Backup & more in the popup, and Marginal switches to it automatically when the main one hits a limit or fails. Ollama on your own computer has no limits.

Is my draft private?

Marginal has no servers. Your draft goes only to the provider you chose, or stays on your computer with Ollama. Read the details in the privacy policy.
