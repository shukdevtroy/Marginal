WHAT IT CATCHES

Four kinds of feedback, one pass.

Every analysis returns the same shape of notes an experienced editor would leave — nothing generic, nothing you have to decode.

Grammar issues

Specific mistakes, each with the correction and a plain explanation of why it's wrong — not just a red underline. "was suppose to" → "were supposed to" — past modal needs the base verb

Word suggestions

Stronger, more precise word choices swapped in where your phrasing is vague, weak, or overused. "a lot of details" → "several open questions"

Sentence suggestions

Full rewrites for sentences that ran long, buried the point, or tried to do two things at once.

Tone & professionalism

Notes on politeness and register, plus overall / professional / clarity scores so you can see the gap at a glance.

HOW IT WORKS

Built to stay out of your way.

01

You type

Open a compose window in Gmail and start writing. Marginal slides in beside it.

02

You pause

About two seconds after you stop — not every keystroke — analysis quietly kicks off in the background.

03

Notes appear

Scores, grammar fixes, and rewrites land in the panel beside your draft, without freezing it.

04

You decide

Apply fixes one by one, fix all grammar, polish the whole email, or ignore it and keep writing. Ctrl+Z always works.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is Marginal really free?

Marginal itself is free. With Ollama it costs nothing to run. Google AI Studio has a free tier with daily limits, and Hugging Face and OpenRouter offer small free allowances. Hosted providers set their own limits and can change them, so only a local model is free no matter what.

Do you see my emails?

No. There are no Marginal servers, so nothing reaches us. Your draft goes straight from your browser to the provider you chose, or stays on your computer with Ollama.

Does it read my whole inbox?

No. It looks only at the compose window you have open, and leaves out your signature and quoted replies.

Will it change my email without asking?

Never. Suggestions wait for you. Apply them one at a time, use Fix all grammar, or preview a polish before you choose Replace draft. Ctrl/⌘+Z in Gmail undoes it.

Which browsers and mail services work?

Chrome and Edge, with Gmail on the web. Other mail services aren't supported.

What if my AI provider runs out of credits?

Set a backup provider under Backup & more. If your main one hits a limit or fails, Marginal switches over automatically.
